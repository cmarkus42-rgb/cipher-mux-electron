## Beschreibung

# Feature Request: Add functionality to collect external tmux/terminal sessions

**Severity:** low
**Tags:** feature-request, tmux, session-management

## Summary
Implement a feature to allow cipher-mux to discover and integrate external, currently running tmux or terminal sessions into its management interface.

## Expected Behavior
The application should provide a mechanism to detect and "collect" external terminal or tmux sessions into the cipher-mux view.

## Actual Behavior
Existing sessions running in external terminal instances are excluded from the cipher-mux session display.
